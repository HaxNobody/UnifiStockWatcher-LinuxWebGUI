The watcher sends an alert to a topic on your ntfy server. The ntfy app on your phone subscribes to that same server and topic.

          

            Setup guide: connect your phone
            
    
              
  1. Choose ntfy.sh or your own server. The simplest option is https://ntfy.sh. No account is required for basic public topics. If you want an account, open Create an ntfy account, choose a username and password, and follow the prompts. This is a separate ntfy account, not a watcher login.
    2. 
              
  2. Install ntfy on your phone. Get it from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). Open the app and allow notifications.
    3. 
              
  3. Choose your server and topic below. A topic is the name of your notification channel. Use Generate topic for a random name, or enter your own. You do not need to create a public topic elsewhere first.
    4. 
              
  4. Subscribe on your phone. In ntfy, tap + or Subscribe to topic. Use exactly the server address and topic shown here, then subscribe. Keep the topic's spelling and capitalization the same. Merely signing into an account does not subscribe the phone.

    5. 
              
  5. Save and test. Choose your alert options, then use Save settings & send test. Look for the test on your phone. A successful send confirms that ntfy accepted it; only your phone can confirm receipt. You can close this page afterward; the Linux watcher keeps running.
    6. 
            

            
Topic privacy: anyone who knows an unprotected topic can read or publish messages. Creating an account alone does not protect it. For a protected topic, reserve/configure its permissions in ntfy, create an access token in your ntfy account, enter that token below, and sign into the appropriate server account on your phone. A suitable ntfy plan may be required. A token authenticates the watcher; topic permissions control access.

            
Own server, account-protected topics, and troubleshooting
              
For your own ntfy server, use its base address on both the watcher and phone. Both devices must reach it. If it is reachable only over Tailscale, connect the phone to Tailscale too. Public ntfy.sh alerts need Internet access, but the phone does not need Tailscale just to receive them.

              
For protected topics, create a dedicated token in your ntfy web account's Account section. The token needs publish access to this topic. A 401 or 403 response usually points to an expired token or topic permissions. Do not put a password or token in the server URL. The token is stored only in the private settings file on the server, not returned to browsers; any person with GUI access can replace it.

              
If a test is accepted but nothing arrives, check the exact server and topic, the phone's notification permission, mute and Do Not Disturb settings, and its network connection. On Android, review ntfy's background or instant-delivery settings if delivery is delayed. Self-hosted iPhone delivery may need upstream push configuration on the ntfy server.
